## ⚙️ Engineering Profile

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer focused on building **autonomous and intelligent systems** at the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, embedded computing, and software architecture.

My engineering approach is centered around one idea:

* 👁️ Computer Vision & Perception

* 🏗️ AI Systems & Software Architecture
* 🌐 Distributed & Real-Time Systems
